Install on iPhone / iPad

To use Shape Squad offline on your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

The app will now launch directly from your home screen and run completely offline without cellular or Wi-Fi!
